# E-Governance Data Analysis Plan

**Week 1 research and planning deliverable proposing a data analysis strategy — objectives, data sources, KPIs, analytical techniques, and an 8-month roadmap — for evidence-based decision-making in e-governance and digital public services.**

## Objective

This repository documents a research-backed data analysis strategy for E-Governance and Digital Services. It reviews current global and national trends (UN E-Government Survey 2024, OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026, and India's Digital Public Infrastructure ecosystem), identifies the key gaps limiting data-driven public administration, and proposes a phased plan to close them.

## Key Findings from the Literature Review

- Global e-government development is rising sharply (EGDI average up from 0.6102 to 0.6382, 2022→2024), but the **analytics layer lags behind the infrastructure layer** — most investment has gone into front-end digitization, not back-end insight generation.
- Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) — digital identity, payments, and data exchange — is now the dominant e-governance paradigm, but suffers from interoperability gaps, unclear data ownership, and privacy/trust deficits.
- Predictive and prescriptive analytics adoption in government remains held back by poor data quality, siloed records, and a lack of pre-implementation baselines.

## The Proposed Data Analysis Plan

| Element | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Objectives** | Unify fragmented data, embed privacy-by-design, build a tiered (descriptive → prescriptive) analytics capability, close equity gaps, and create a citizen-feedback → policy loop |
| **Data Sources** | Citizen portals, administrative registries, grievance systems, geospatial/IoT feeds, open-data portals, licensed social listening |
| **Key Metrics** | 10 KPIs across efficiency, equity, data quality, trust, financial integrity, predictive performance, and policy impact |
| **Analytical Techniques** | Descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics |
| **Timeline** | 8-month, 7-phase roadmap — governance & data quality first, modelling second, pilot and scale last |

See `/report/E-Governance_Data_Initiatives_Report.docx` for the full write-up.
